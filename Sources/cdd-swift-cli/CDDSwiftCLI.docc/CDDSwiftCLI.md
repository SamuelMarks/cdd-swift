# ``cdd_swift_cli``

Command-line interface and programmatic entrypoint for `cdd-swift`.

## Overview

`cdd-swift-cli` provides the `cdd-swift` executable and `CDDCLI` programmatic entrypoints for compiling OpenAPI 3.2.0, Swagger 2.0, and Arazzo 1.1.0 specifications to and from Swift 6 SDKs, CLIs, Vapor servers, tests, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) endpoints across macOS, Linux, Windows, and WASI.
